What is FBTA?

FBTA is a fungible token on the Solana blockchain. Today you can hold it, send it, and swap it in any Solana wallet.

The goal is to make FBTA useful in real transportation: paying for trips, settling between providers, and rewarding riders and drivers. Those features are planned and not live yet.
